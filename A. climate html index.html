What ClimateGuard is

ClimateGuard is an educational awareness website built to explain climate basics clearly and encourage simple everyday habits. It is not a live carbon calculator, emissions database, or forecasting tool.

Your checklist progress is stored locally in your own browser using localStorage—no account, tracking, or server is required.
